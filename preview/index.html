About

Geographically structured freshwater fishes reveal how lineages diverged and how river-basin history shaped present-day diversity.

I work from field collections and specimen-linked material, combining morphology, molecular data and GIS to resolve species boundaries and reconstruct biogeographic history.

The same well-documented systems can be revisited as new evolutionary questions and analytical approaches emerge.
