Research

Field-based ichthyology built around populations, specimens and geographic variation.

My work focuses on freshwater fish diversity and evolutionary history, combining field observations, morphology and molecular evidence.

I am also interested in broader comparative questions and complementary approaches that can extend what these systems can tell us.
