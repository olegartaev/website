How do geography and environment shape the diversity and evolution of freshwater fishes?

Research

Freshwater diversity in geographic context.

Current work focuses on integrative taxonomy, phylogeography and biogeography of freshwater fishes, with Phoxinus providing a geographically broad comparative system across Eurasia.

01

Integrative taxonomy

Species boundaries and diversity assessed from specimens, external morphology and molecular evidence.

02

Phylogeography & biogeography

Drainage history, isolation and geographic structure used to interpret the distribution of freshwater lineages.

03

Geographic variation

Morphological and genetic variation compared across populations and contrasting freshwater systems.

The site shows established work and current methods. Emerging genomic and analytical directions are not presented here as completed capabilities.
