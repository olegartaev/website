Current research

Phoxinus of Eastern Europe and Asia

Across Eastern Europe and Asia, some Phoxinus lineages combine deep mitochondrial divergence with striking phenotypic similarity, geographic isolation and complex river-basin histories. Current work examines these patterns across drainage systems, including cases where mitochondrial history alone does not resolve lineage relationships.
